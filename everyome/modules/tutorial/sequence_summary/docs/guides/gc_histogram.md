**What it shows.** How many sequences have each GC content (the share of bases that are G or C).
Dark bars are inside the range you keep.

- **One peak** is usual for sequences from one organism; its position is that organism's typical GC content.
- **Two peaks** can mean two organisms (contamination, or a host and a symbiont) or two kinds of sequence.
- **A very wide spread** often comes from short sequences: with few bases, GC content varies a lot by chance.
