**Genes detected** counts genes at 1 TPM or more in that sample. **Top 10 genes' share** is the percentage of all the
sample's TPM taken by its ten most expressed genes.

- Samples of one group should detect similar numbers of genes.
- A sample whose top ten genes take a much larger share than the others (for example mostly ribosomal or
  mitochondrial transcripts) has fewer reads left for everything else; its other genes are measured less precisely.
