**What it shows.** Each sample placed by its overall expression: principal components of the most variable genes,
after normalising for library size.

- **Samples of one group should sit together.** Groups that separate along PC1 differ in many genes.
- A sample sitting with another group may be mislabelled; one far from everything may have failed.
- If samples group by the day they were prepared or sequenced instead of by group, that batch effect will show in
  every later chart too.
