**What it shows.** One gene: its log2 TE in each Ribo-seq/RNA-seq pair, coloured by condition, with the normalised
footprints and mRNA reads behind it, and its predicted ORFs.

- Pairs of one condition should agree. One pair far from the rest can drive a result on its own.
- A uORF on a gene whose TE changed is worth a look: uORFs are a common way translation is regulated.
