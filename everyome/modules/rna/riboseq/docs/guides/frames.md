**What it shows.** For each Ribo-seq library, the share of P-sites in coding sequences that fall in the annotated reading
frame (frame 0).

- Ribosomes move one codon (3 nt) at a time, so real footprints pile up in one frame. **50–80% in frame 0** is typical
  of a good library; **33%** means no rhythm at all.
- A low value can mean the P-site offsets are wrong for some lengths, the digestion was uneven, or many reads are not
  footprints (degraded mRNA, rRNA fragments). Check the table of lengths for this library.
- Libraries with a much weaker rhythm than the rest give less reliable counts and ORF calls.
