**What it shows.** P-sites of all footprints at each position around the start codon (left) and stop codon (right),
summed over genes. Bars are coloured by reading frame.

- **A repeating tall–short–short pattern** inside the coding sequence is the 3-nt rhythm of translation.
- A **peak at the start codon** comes from initiating ribosomes; drugs such as harringtonine make it much larger.
- **Few P-sites after the stop codon** is expected: ribosomes leave there. Many reads in 3' UTRs suggest
  contamination or reinitiation.
- Before the start codon (5' leader), reads without frame often come from upstream ORFs, in their own frame.
