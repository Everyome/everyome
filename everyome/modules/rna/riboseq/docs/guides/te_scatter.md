**What it shows.** Every tested gene placed by how its mRNA changed (RNA-seq, across) and how its footprints changed
(Ribo-seq, up), between the two conditions.

- **On the diagonal**: footprints follow the mRNA. These genes change in **mRNA abundance** (if they move at all), with
  the same translational efficiency.
- **Off the diagonal**: footprints changed more or less than the mRNA, so **translation** itself changed. Above the
  diagonal, TE went up; below it, down.
- **Buffering**: the mRNA changed but footprints did not follow (near the horizontal axis), so TE changed in the
  opposite direction and protein output may stay the same.
