**What it shows.** How many proteins matched anything, an InterPro entry, or a GO term; matches per member database;
and the entries found in most proteins.

- For a well-studied group of organisms, most proteins match something. For a newly assembled genome, a lower share is
  normal: lineage-specific genes and fragments of mispredicted genes match nothing.
- Each database has its own idea of a domain, so one domain often gives several matches; InterPro groups them into one
  entry.
