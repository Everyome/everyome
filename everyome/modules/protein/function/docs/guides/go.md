**What it shows.** Gene Ontology terms implied by the InterPro entries your proteins match, in three aspects:
**molecular function** (what the protein does), **biological process** (what it takes part in) and **cellular
component** (where).

- These terms are inferred from domains, not measured: a protein kinase domain implies kinase activity, which a
  particular protein may have lost.
- For enrichment tests (are some terms over-represented in a gene set?), export go_terms.csv and use an enrichment tool
  with your whole proteome as the background.
