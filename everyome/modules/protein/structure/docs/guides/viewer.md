**How to use it.** Each residue is shown by its central carbon atom (Cα), joined in sequence. Drag to turn the
structure, click it and scroll to zoom (or hold Ctrl or ⌘ while scrolling), and point at a residue to see its number, amino acid and pLDDT.

- Colour by **pLDDT** (blue very high, light blue confident, yellow low, orange very low, the colours most
  structure databases use), by **domain** or by **chain**.
- Low-confidence regions often look like long loose loops or 'spaghetti': that shape means nothing.
- For a full view with side chains and surfaces, open the exported PDB in ChimeraX, PyMOL or Mol*.
