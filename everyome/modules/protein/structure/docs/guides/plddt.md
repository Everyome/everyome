**What it shows.** pLDDT, the predictor's confidence in each residue's local structure, from 0 to 100.

- **Above 90** (very high): side chains are usually right too. **70–90** (confident): the backbone is usually right.
  **50–70** (low): treat with caution. **Below 50** (very low): not a structure; often a disordered region.
- Low confidence is not always a failure: many proteins have disordered tails, linkers or whole regions that
  only fold when bound to a partner.
- Lines for the lower-ranked models show how consistent the predictor was. Regions where they disagree are less
  certain than the top model alone suggests.
