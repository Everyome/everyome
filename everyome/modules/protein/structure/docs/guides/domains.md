**How Everyome finds them.** Stretches of at least five residues with pLDDT 70 or above are taken first; stretches
are then joined when the mean PAE between them (both ways) is below 5 Å. Groups of at least 20 residues are listed
as domains. This is a simple, readable rule, not a replacement for a domain database: two domains that pack
firmly together come out as one.

- Domain boundaries are a good start for designing constructs (for example, to express one domain on its own).
- A protein with no domains listed has no part that is both confident and rigid: often a disordered protein.
