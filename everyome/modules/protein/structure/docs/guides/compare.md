**What it shows.** How similar the same protein's structures from different methods are, as the RMSD (root-mean-square
distance, in Å) between their Cα atoms after the best superposition.

- **Below about 2 Å**: the same fold. **Above about 4 Å**: different structures, or the same domains arranged
  differently.
- Compare the RMSD over **confident residues** too: disordered regions always differ and say nothing.
- If each domain agrees but the whole does not, the methods place the domains differently, which the PAE usually
  warns about. Agreement between methods is reassuring but not proof: they learnt from the same structures.
