**What it shows.** Molecules (UMIs) and genes per cell, with your thresholds as dashed lines.

- **Below the lower lines** are near-empty barcodes: ambient RNA, not cells.
- **Above the upper lines** are likely doublets: two cells captured with one barcode.
- In the scatter, red points are removed. Real cells form a tight diagonal band.
- Set upper bounds where the long right tail starts, not in the middle of the main peak.
