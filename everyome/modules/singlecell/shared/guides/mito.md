**What it shows.** For each cell, the share of its molecules that come from mitochondrial genes.

- Most healthy cells sit in a low peak (often under 5–10%, higher in muscle, heart and kidney).
- A long tail or second peak at high values usually means **damaged or dying cells**: their cytoplasmic RNA leaked out, leaving the mitochondrial RNA behind.
- Set the threshold where the tail starts. Too strict a cut removes real, highly active cell types; check which clusters lose most cells.
- No mitochondrial genes found? The gene names may not start with MT- or mt-; change the *Mitochondrial genes* setting.
