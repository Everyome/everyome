**What it shows.** Genes higher in this cluster than in all others.

- **log FC** is log2 fold change: 1 means twice as high, 3 means eight times.
- **In / out** is the share of cells expressing the gene inside and outside the cluster. Good markers are high in, low out.
- Name a cluster from several markers together, using known cell-type markers for your tissue (for example CD3E for T cells, MS4A1 for B cells in blood). Mitochondrial or ribosomal genes as top markers often mean stressed or low-quality cells.
