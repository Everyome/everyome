**What it shows.** Where aligned reads land in the genome annotation.

- **Coding and UTR** reads are the mRNA signal you count. Higher is better.
- **Intronic** reads come from unspliced RNA. Some are normal; very many can mean nuclear RNA or degradation.
- **Intergenic** reads and **NO_XF** (no annotation) can mean genomic DNA, an incomplete annotation, or poor alignment.
- Sublibraries from the same experiment should look alike.
