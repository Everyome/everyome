**What it shows.** Cells, unique molecules (UMIs) and reads per UMI for each sublibrary.

- **Reads per UMI** is how often each molecule was sequenced. Above about 10, sequencing more adds few new molecules;
  below about 2, more sequencing would find many more.
- **Cells** should be near what you loaded; far more can mean background barcodes counted as cells.
