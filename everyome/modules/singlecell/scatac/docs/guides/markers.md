**What it shows.** Genes whose **activity** (open chromatin over the gene and 2 kb upstream) is higher in this
cluster than in all others.

- Gene activity is an estimate of expression from chromatin; it is noisier than RNA, and open is not always
  expressed. Name clusters from several known markers together.
- **log FC** is log2 fold change: 1 means twice as high. **In / out** is the share of cells with any activity inside
  and outside the cluster.
