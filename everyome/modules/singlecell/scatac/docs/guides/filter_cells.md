**What it shows.** Fragments and TSS enrichment per cell, with your thresholds as dashed lines.

- **Few fragments**: barcodes with little DNA, often empty droplets or debris.
- **Low TSS enrichment** (below about 4): little signal at gene starts, so mostly background; often dying cells or
  ambient DNA. ENCODE asks for at least 4 to 6.
- **Very many fragments**: possibly two cells sharing a barcode.
- In the scatter, red points are removed. Real cells form a cloud with many fragments and high TSS enrichment.
