**What it shows.** How long each sample's fragments are. A good library shows a **ladder**: a tall peak below about
100 bp (open chromatin, between nucleosomes), then bumps at about 200 and 400 bp (DNA wrapped around one and two
nucleosomes).

- No ladder, or mostly long fragments, suggests over-digested or degraded nuclei, or poor size selection.
- Samples prepared the same way should have the same shape.
