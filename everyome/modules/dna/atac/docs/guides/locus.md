**What it shows.** One region of the genome. From the top: genes (arrow = direction), the consensus peaks (coloured
when the comparison found them more or less open), and each library's own peaks, with height showing how strong the
peak is (or read coverage, when bigWigs can be read).

- A real change shows in every replicate of a group, not in one library alone.
- A peak at a gene's start is a promoter; peaks inside or between genes are often enhancers.
- Make the region wider to see neighbouring peaks and genes.
