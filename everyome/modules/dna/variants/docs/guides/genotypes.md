**What it shows.** Every caller's call for one variant in every sample, with the reads behind it.

- **Ref reads** and **Alt reads** count reads showing the reference and the variant. One copy gives roughly half and
  half; two copies give nearly all variant reads.
- **GQ** is how sure the caller is of the genotype; **QUAL** how sure it is the variant exists.
- When callers disagree, the read counts usually show why: few reads, or a split that could be either genotype.
