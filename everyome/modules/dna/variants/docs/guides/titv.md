**What it shows.** What each caller found in each sample before any filter.

- **Ti/Tv** is the ratio of transitions (A↔G, C↔T) to transversions among SNVs. Real variants in most animals give about
  2 across a genome (higher in coding exons); random errors give about 0.5. A low ratio means many false calls.
- **Het** is one copy of the variant, **Hom** two. Inbred lines and clones carry mostly two-copy variants; outbred
  animals mostly one-copy.
- Samples of the same kind should have similar numbers. One far off may have low depth or contamination.
