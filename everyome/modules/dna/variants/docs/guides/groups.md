**What it shows.** Variants that every sample of one group carries and no sample of the other does, and where they lie.

- In a mutant screen, the causal mutation is one of these, and the DNA around it, inherited with it, carries more of
  them: look for a **cluster** along one chromosome.
- With few samples, some ordinary variants separate the groups by chance. They are scattered; a real linked region is
  a cluster. More samples per group shrink the chance ones quickly.
- Uncertain genotypes (?) never count as separating; loosen the filters if a region you expect is missing.
