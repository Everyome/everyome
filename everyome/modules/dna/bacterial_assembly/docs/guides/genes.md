**What it shows.** Genes found on this assembly by Prokka or Bakta (from the sequence itself, no reference needed).

- A typical bacterium has roughly one gene per kilobase: about 4,000–5,000 genes for a 4.5 Mb genome.
- Many genes with no known product (**hypothetical protein**) is normal; Bakta's larger database names more of them than Prokka.
- Far fewer genes than expected can mean a fragmented assembly, or many frameshifts from uncorrected long-read errors (polish first).
