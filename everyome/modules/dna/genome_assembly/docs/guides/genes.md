**What it shows.** Genes copied onto this assembly from the related genome you gave (Liftoff).

- **Not lifted over** counts genes that could not be placed: they may cross a break between contigs, sit in sequence the assembly lacks, or have diverged too far in your organism.
- Genes are only as good as the reference annotation, and genes your organism has but the relative lacks are not found this way.
- Many genes not lifted over from a close relative can mean a fragmented assembly; compare assemblies.
