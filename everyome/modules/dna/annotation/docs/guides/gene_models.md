**What it shows.** The predicted genes in numbers.

- **Genes**: compare with a related genome of similar size. Many more can mean fragments of one gene called separately;
  many fewer, genes missed.
- **Exons per gene** and **single-exon genes**: most animal genes have several exons. A large share of single-exon genes
  often means fragments or transposons.
- **Protein length**: most proteins are a few hundred amino acids; a peak of very short ones suggests fragments.
- Helixer predicts from sequence alone. Where you have RNA-seq, check a few genes of interest against it.
