**What it shows.** For each assembly, the contig length (up) against how much of the assembly is in contigs at least that long (across).

- A line that stays **high and flat** for longer is more contiguous. Where it crosses 50% is the N50.
- A line that falls early means most of the genome is in short pieces.
- Lines ending at different points to the right only mean different contig counts; compare their heights.
