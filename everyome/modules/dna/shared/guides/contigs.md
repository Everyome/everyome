**What it shows.** Each contig's length and GC content.

- Contigs from one genome share a similar GC content. A separate cluster at a different GC, especially of short contigs, can be **contamination**, or a **plasmid**, **mitochondrion** or **chloroplast**.
- Check unusual contigs further (for example with BLAST) before removing anything.
- Many very short contigs usually mean low coverage, or repeats the reads could not cross.
