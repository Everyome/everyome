**What it shows.** Transcripts and genes per cell, with your thresholds as dashed lines.

- Panels measure a few hundred genes, so a good cell may have only tens to a few hundred transcripts.
- **Very low counts**: cells cut at the surface of the section, or debris segmented as cells.
- **Very high counts**: often two cells segmented as one; check them on the tissue before removing many.
- In the scatter, red points are removed.
