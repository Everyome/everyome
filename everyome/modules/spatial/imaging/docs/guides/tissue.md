**What it shows.** Every cell in its place, coloured by what you choose. Cells are drawn as dots at their centres
(no outlines); very large sections are thinned for drawing only, the analysis uses every cell.

- Cell types that form layers, patches or tracts are a good sign. Types that follow the edge of the section or a tear
  usually reflect quality, not biology: compare with *Transcripts per cell*.
- Turn *Size* up to see small sections better; 1 draws dots about half the distance between neighbouring cells.
