**What it shows.** Every bin in its place (over the tissue image for Visium HD), coloured by what you choose. Very
large sections are thinned for drawing only; the analysis uses every bin.

- Types that follow visible structures are a good sign. Patterns that follow the edge or a tear usually reflect
  quality: compare with *UMIs per bin*.
- At 16 µm, bins on a boundary mix both sides; at 8 µm they mostly hold one cell.
