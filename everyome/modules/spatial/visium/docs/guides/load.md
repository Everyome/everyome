**What Everyome reads.** From each section's Space Ranger `outs` folder: the counts for spots under the tissue
(`filtered_feature_bc_matrix`), where each spot sits (`spatial/tissue_positions.csv`) and the tissue image.

- Spots off the tissue are left out, as Space Ranger marked them.
- If a section looks wrong on the image (spots off the tissue, or the image flipped), check Space Ranger's alignment
  (or redo it in Loupe Browser) before going on.
