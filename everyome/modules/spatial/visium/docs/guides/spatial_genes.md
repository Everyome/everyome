**What it shows.** Genes ranked by Moran's I: how similar a gene's expression is in neighbouring spots.

- Near **0**: no spatial pattern (expression looks scattered). Near **1**: expression forms smooth patches or layers.
- Genes that mark a structure (a layer, a region) score high. So can genes affected by tissue quality, such as
  mitochondrial genes near a damaged edge: look at them on the tissue before drawing conclusions.
- Moran's I depends on how many spots express the gene; very rare genes score low even if they form a small patch.
