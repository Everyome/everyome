**What it shows.** Each spot in its place on the tissue image, coloured by what you choose.

- A Visium spot is 55 µm across and usually holds several cells (often 1–10), so a spot's expression is a mixture. Spots
  on a boundary mix both sides.
- Clusters that follow visible structures (layers, glands, a lesion) are a good sign. Clusters that follow the edge of the
  section or a fold usually reflect quality, not biology: compare with *UMIs per spot*.
- Lower the spot opacity to see the tissue under the spots. *Spot size* 1 draws spots at their true size; larger is only easier to see.
