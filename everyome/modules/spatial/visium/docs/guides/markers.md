**What it shows.** Genes higher in this cluster of spots than in all others.

- **log FC** is log2 fold change: 1 means twice as high, 3 means eight times.
- **In / out** is the share of spots expressing the gene inside and outside the cluster.
- Because spots mix cells, a region's markers can come from several cell types (for example tumour cells and the immune
  cells around them). Name regions by tissue structure, using the tissue view alongside the markers.
