**What it shows.** For each pair of types, how often they are neighbours compared with chance. The labels are
shuffled many times within each section; each square's colour is how many times more (or less) often the pair are
neighbours than in the shuffles, on a log scale. Hover for the number and its z-score.

- **Strong colour on the diagonal**: that type forms patches or layers of its own.
- **Positive or near zero off the diagonal**: the two types sit together, for example a layer and the layer beside it,
  or immune cells gathered around a structure.
- **Strongly negative**: they rarely or never touch, for example layers far apart.
- Large tissues make even small differences "significant" (large z-scores). Judge by the size of the effect and by the
  tissue itself.
