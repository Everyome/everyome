**What it shows.** How much each target changes in each group compared with the control, after normalising to the
reference genes.

- **Fold change** 2 means twice as much; 0.5 means half. The dots show each biological replicate on a log2 scale, where
  +1 is double and −1 is half.
- **95% CI**: the range the true fold change probably lies in. A range that includes 1 means no clear change.
- **p** comes from Welch's t-test on log2 values; **adjusted p** corrects for testing several targets at once.
- With three replicates per group, only large changes reach significance. Report the fold change and its interval, not
  just the p-value.
