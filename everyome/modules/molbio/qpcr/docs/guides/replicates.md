**What it shows.** Sets of technical replicates (same sample, same target) whose Cq values spread more than the limit
in the run's settings, or where a well gave no Cq.

- Technical replicates usually agree within 0.2–0.3 cycles. One cycle is a two-fold difference in template.
- When one well of three sits apart from the other two, Everyome suggests leaving it out. Do this only if there is a
  reason to distrust the well, not to tidy the data.
- A whole set that spreads widely often means very little template (Cq above about 32), where counting noise takes over.
