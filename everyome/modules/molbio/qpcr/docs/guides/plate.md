**What it shows.** Every well on the plate, coloured by its Cq: darker wells crossed the threshold earlier, so they
started with more template. Open circles gave no Cq.

- Gold rings are standards, dark rings are no-template controls.
- A well that stands out from its neighbours (same sample and target) may have had a pipetting error or a bubble.
- Leaving a well out is recorded, and named in the methods text.
