**What it shows.** Wells with no template, which should give no signal.

- **clean**: no Cq, as it should be.
- **late signal**: a Cq after the cut-off in the settings. With SYBR Green this is usually primer dimer; check the melt
  curve on the instrument. It matters if your samples' Cq values come close to it.
- **contaminated**: an earlier Cq means template got into the well. Results for that target are doubtful until it is
  repeated with fresh reagents.
