**What it shows.** Primer pairs Primer3 suggests, best first.

- **Tm** (melting temperature) of the two primers should be within 2–3 °C of each other.
- **GC** between about 40% and 60% binds reliably.
- **Penalty** only ranks how closely each pair fits your settings; lower is better.
- The **product** must cover your region; check its size suits your gel or downstream use.
