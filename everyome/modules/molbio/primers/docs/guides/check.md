**What it shows.** Thermodynamic checks for your own primers, using your reaction conditions.

- **Hairpin** and **dimer ΔG** say how strongly a primer folds on itself or sticks to its partner.
  Closer to 0 is better; below about −9 kcal/mol (in red) can stop the PCR working.
- **Binds this sequence** is an exact match only. Check other templates, such as the genome, separately.
